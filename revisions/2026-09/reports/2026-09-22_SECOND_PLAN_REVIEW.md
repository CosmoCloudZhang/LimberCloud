## Source checks supporting the changes

- The six spectra notebooks' numerical routines interpolate radial inputs and integrate NN as effective power times both densities divided by chi squared. If those densities are nonzero constants at zero and power is proportional to chi, the integrand is 1/chi and its observer integral diverges. This concerns the observer, independently of allowing a nonzero far-endpoint coefficient.
- `experiments/spectra/NUMBA/Y1/triple.py` around lines 197–204 constructs the observer k with infinity, passes it through `numpy.nan_to_num`, and evaluates the power provider at every radial node. The new prompt explicitly replaces this limiting procedure, not the physical positive-node power model.
- `scripts/generate_config/intrinsic_alignment.py` and `galaxy_bias.py` around lines 32–46 pass Omega_g and CAMB kmax=100. `src/limbercloud/validation/samples.py` around lines 258–280 omits Omega_g and uses kmax=50. This was already noted in the first science audit but was not sufficiently concrete in the executable prompts. A settings difference does not by itself prove a difference in D(z), H(z), chi(z) or rho_m(0); the new checks establish the effective relationship.
- `manuscript/sections/section3.tex` line 58 states a per-component ellipticity dispersion of 0.26. The covariance prompt now states the corresponding variance convention and independent unit checks rather than relying on an unspecified upstream interpretation.
- The benchmark plotting entries are `experiments/benchmarks/Y1/benchmark.py` and `experiments/benchmarks/Y10/benchmark.py`, with `figure.sh` launchers. They are scripts, not additional benchmark notebooks.
