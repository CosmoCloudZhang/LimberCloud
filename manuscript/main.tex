\abstract{Next-generation imaging surveys demand rapid and accurate evaluation of angular power spectra across high-dimensional parameter spaces. We present \texttt{LimberCloud}, a tensorised reformulation of the Limber integral that algebraically separates cosmology-dependent quantities from survey-specific tomographic redshift distributions. The factorisation reduces each line-of-sight integral to closed-form expressions, eliminating numerical quadrature entirely. Validated against the Core Cosmology Library for the LSST Y1 \(3\times2\,\mathrm{pt}\) data vector, the method achieves sub-per-cent accuracy across all tomographic bin pairs and multipoles \(20 \le \ell \le 2000\). A GPU-accelerated \texttt{JAX} implementation evaluates the full set of angular power spectra in approximately \(3\,\mathrm{ms}\)---over three orders of magnitude faster than conventional approaches---and the cosmology-dependent coefficients are structured for end-to-end emulation, offering a further three-to-four order-of-magnitude speed-up at inference time. The algebraic structure also reveals that the predicted spectra depend on the redshift distribution uncertainties as an exact low-order polynomial, enabling analytic marginalisation that avoids the finite-difference and fixed-cosmology approximations of existing treatments. Extensions to non-flat geometries, CMB lensing, and beyond-Limber scales are outlined.}
